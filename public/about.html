About Lambton Capital.

A commercial finance brokerage based in Newcastle, working with owner-managed businesses and private investors across Australia.

Why Lambton Capital exists

Lambton Capital was founded by Thomas Coppen, who spent years on the other side of the table: as the owner of a marketing agency, and later a marketing group.

“Over that time I borrowed for almost every reason a business can. Loans to buy other companies as the group grew. Loans to keep the business running through COVID. Loans to expand, to buy equipment, and to open offices in Dubai and the USA.

Working with brokers was often frustrating. Updates were slow, or didn’t come at all. It wasn’t always clear why a particular lender had been recommended, or how the broker was being paid for recommending it. Deals stalled for weeks. On some deals, I found better quotes through my own research than the broker brought me.

Even the introductions weren’t always what they seemed. The brokers my accountants referred me to weren’t always the right fit for my business, and I only found out later that some of those introductions came with a referral fee paid back to the person making them.

Lambton Capital is built to fix those things. Our fees, and how lenders pay us, are published. Every enquiry gets a written assessment that explains why each lender is on the shortlist. If anyone is paid for referring you to us, we tell you who and how much before you go ahead. You deal with one broker from enquiry to settlement, and you hear from us at every stage without having to chase.”

Thomas also brings a background in performance marketing, a business degree and a Master’s in International Business Studies, so deals are assessed with a numbers-first approach. He is based in Newcastle, New South Wales, and works with clients Australia-wide. Deals are handled remotely end to end, with site visits arranged only where a lender requires one.

Why we publish our fees

Most brokers don't publish what they charge. We do, because a borrower comparing lenders shouldn't have to guess what the broker side of the deal costs. Our fee schedule sets out three tiers — $0 on standard deals, 1.25% on structured deals, 1.75% on specialist deals — all payable at settlement only (when the loan funds are paid and the deal completes), with no engagement fee and no application fee. If a deal doesn't settle, there's no fee. See the full fee schedule for how each tier applies and worked examples.

How we work

Written assessments

Every enquiry gets a written assessment of how lenders are likely to view the deal, not a verbal opinion you have to take notes on. It's something you can share with your accountant or business partner.

One broker, end to end

The person who takes your enquiry is the person who manages the application through to settlement. No handoffs between a sales team and a processing team.

Every lender choice explained

The assessment sets out why each lender is on the shortlist and what that lender would pay us, so you can weigh the recommendation knowing exactly how we’re paid.

Updates without chasing

You hear from us at each stage: submission, approval, valuation, loan documents and settlement. If a lender goes quiet, we follow it up, not you.
