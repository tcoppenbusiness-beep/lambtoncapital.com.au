Deals we arrange

Commercial finance we arrange, with the numbers.

Illustrative structures showing how a deal is put together. Real, anonymised deal notes replace these from our first settlement.

Example structure — illustrative

Industrial purchase, Beresfield NSW

Situation: A trades business buying the 900 m² unit it already operates from, held in a trust with a corporate trustee.

Constraint: Only 18 months of trading history under the current structure — not enough for a major bank.

Structure: Non-bank lender, 65% LVR, 25-year term repaying principal and interest.

Loan $1.4m · LVR 65% · Lender tier Non-bank · Days to settle 31

Example structure — illustrative

Medical suite refinance, Parramatta NSW

Situation: A medical practice whose loan was due to expire in six weeks, after a big-four bank changed its interest rate on renewal.

Constraint: Little time to refinance before the existing loan lapsed.

Structure: Mid-tier bank (smaller than the big four), 60% LVR.

Loan $2.1m · LVR 60% · Lender tier Mid-tier bank · Days to settle 24

Example structure — illustrative

SMSF warehouse purchase, Dandenong VIC

Situation: An owner-operator's self-managed super fund buying the premises the operating business leases.

Constraint: The fund had limited cash, which capped the deposit and cash buffer it could commit.

Structure: Limited-recourse loan, 60% LVR.

Loan $1.1m · LVR 60% · Lender tier Specialist SMSF lender · Days to settle 38
