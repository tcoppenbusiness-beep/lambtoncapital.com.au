Privacy policy.

Last updated 28 September 2026.

Our commitment

Lambton Capital Pty Ltd (ABN 51 703 121 288) handles personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

We collect identity, contact, business and financial information needed to assess and arrange finance on your behalf, including details you give us in an enquiry form, over email or in a call. We also collect basic website analytics.

How we collect it

Directly from you, through our enquiry form, email and calls; from credit reporting bodies, with your consent; and from third parties such as your accountant, with your consent.

Why we collect it

To assess, arrange and manage your finance application; to comply with our legal and regulatory obligations; and, where you don't opt out, for marketing about our services.

Who we disclose it to

We may disclose your information to lenders, the aggregator we are appointed under, once appointed (the broker group whose licence we will operate under), valuers, solicitors, credit reporting bodies and IT service providers, as needed to assess and arrange your finance.

Credit reporting

We may obtain a commercial credit report (a record of your or your business's credit history) as part of assessing an application. You can request access to, or correction of, information we hold about you at any time.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

Access and correction

You can ask us to access or correct the personal information we hold about you by contacting us through our contact form.

Complaints

If you have a complaint about how we've handled your personal information, see our complaints policy (our internal dispute resolution process). You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Marketing opt-out

You can opt out of marketing communications from us at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email, or by contacting us through our contact form.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Contact us

To ask a question about this policy or your information, use our contact form. We don't take enquiries by phone.
