Deals we arrange

Commercial finance we arrange, with the numbers.

Illustrative structures showing how a deal is put together. Real, anonymised deal notes replace these from our first settlement.

Example structure — illustrative

Industrial unit, Mayfield West

Situation: An earthmoving contractor making its first commercial property purchase.

Constraint: No commercial trading history to lean on, so the deal was also secured against residential property the borrower already owned (cross-collateralised).

Structure: Non-bank lender, 70% LVR.

Loan $960k · LVR 70% · Lender tier Non-bank · Days to settle 27

Example structure — illustrative

Childcare centre purchase, Maitland

Situation: An investor buying a leased childcare centre operating under a 12-year lease.

Constraint: Specialised property type that most lenders assess and lend against differently to standard commercial property.

Structure: Mid-tier bank, 60% LVR against the specialised asset.

Loan $3.2m · LVR 60% · Lender tier Mid-tier bank · Days to settle 41

Example structure — illustrative

Truck yard refinance, Cessnock

Situation: A transport operator consolidating two loans and an Australian Taxation Office (ATO) payment plan into one loan.

Constraint: The existing ATO payment plan narrowed which lenders would consider the deal.

Structure: Specialist lender (focused on harder-to-place deals), 55% LVR.

Loan $1.6m · LVR 55% · Lender tier Specialist lender · Days to settle 35

What we're seeing in the Hunter

Hunter industrial vacancy

Few industrial units sit empty across the Hunter, which keeps prices and rents firm. For a business buying its own premises, that makes the repayment test tighter.

Port and logistics demand

Newcastle's port and logistics corridor continues to draw warehouse and distribution buyers seeking premises finance.

Trades equipment market

Local trades businesses are financing fleet and equipment upgrades as older assets reach replacement age.
