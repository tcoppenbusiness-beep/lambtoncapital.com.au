Commercial property finance

Example structure — illustrative

Industrial purchase, Beresfield NSW

Situation: A trades business buying the 900 m² unit it already operates from, held in a trust whose trustee is a company.

Constraint: Only 18 months of trading history under the current structure — not enough for a major bank.

Structure: Lender that isn't a bank, 65% LVR (loan-to-value ratio — the loan as a percentage of the property's value), 25-year term repaying both interest and the loan itself.

Loan $1.4m · LVR 65% · Lender tier Non-bank · Days to settle 31

Example structure — illustrative

Medical suite refinance, Parramatta NSW

Situation: A medical practice with a facility (the loan or credit line itself) expiring in six weeks, after one of the four major banks changed its rate on renewal.

Constraint: Little time to refinance before the existing facility lapsed.

Structure: Loan from a mid-tier bank (smaller than the big four), 60% LVR.

Loan $2.1m · LVR 60% · Lender tier Mid-tier bank · Days to settle 24

Example structure — illustrative

SMSF warehouse purchase, Dandenong VIC

Situation: An owner-operator's self-managed super fund (SMSF) buying the premises the operating business leases.

Constraint: The fund had limited cash available, which limited the deposit and cash buffer it could commit.

Structure: Limited recourse loan (the special loan type a self-managed super fund must use), 60% LVR.

Loan $1.1m · LVR 60% · Lender tier Specialist SMSF lender · Days to settle 38

SMSF commercial property loans

Example structure — illustrative

Warehouse purchase, Kotara NSW

Situation: A member's fund buying the 650 m² warehouse the member's trading company already leases, with the fund leasing it back to the member's own company at market rent.

Constraint: The fund had the deposit but little spare cash for stamp duty, the bare trust set-up (a separate trust that holds the property until the loan is repaid) and the lender's cash buffer.

Structure: Limited recourse loan from a specialist SMSF lender at 60% LVR, company as trustee, 15-year term, and the lease documented on normal commercial terms (at arm's length).

Loan $660k · LVR 60% · Lender tier Specialist SMSF · Days to settle 38

Example structure — illustrative

Medical suite purchase, Charlestown NSW

Situation: A fund buying a strata medical suite (a unit in a shared building) with an existing unrelated tenant on a five-year lease.

Constraint: Several lenders' rules for self-managed super funds don't cover strata medical suites; the fund's contribution history was short.

Structure: A mid-tier bank's loan for self-managed super funds at 65% LVR, interest only for two years then repaying interest and the loan itself, with rental income covering the repayments without member contributions.

Loan $507k · LVR 65% · Lender tier Mid-tier bank · Days to settle 33

Business finance

Example structure — illustrative

Working capital facility, Wollongong NSW

Situation: A wholesaler needing stock funding ahead of a seasonal peak, trading for two years under the current business structure.

Constraint: Cash flow was seasonal, and a standard assessment for an overdraft (a credit limit on the business account) undervalued it.

Structure: Credit line you can draw, repay and draw again, from a lender that isn't a bank, secured by a general security agreement (the lender's claim over the business's assets if the loan isn't repaid), 12-month review.

Loan $650k · LVR n/a · Lender tier Non-bank · Days to settle 18

Example structure — illustrative

Business acquisition, Bendigo VIC

Situation: A trades operator buying out a competitor's client book and equipment on retirement.

Constraint: Goodwill (the value of the business beyond its physical assets) made up most of the purchase price, which limited how many banks would lend.

Structure: Loan from a mid-tier bank, repaid over 10 years, secured by both the business and a property.

Loan $1.2m · LVR 55% · Lender tier Mid-tier bank · Days to settle 42

Example structure — illustrative

Debt restructure, Toowoomba QLD

Situation: A manufacturer carrying three separate facilities across two lenders after a period of rapid growth.

Constraint: Several loans were backed by overlapping assets and their terms didn't line up, adding cost and complexity.

Structure: One loan from a lender that isn't a bank, replacing the existing debt, 7-year term, one set of assets as security.

Loan $2.4m · LVR 60% · Lender tier Non-bank · Days to settle 35

Business acquisition finance

Example structure — illustrative

Trades business acquisition, Geelong VIC

Situation: A tradesperson buying an established business on the owner's retirement, including machinery, vehicles and a client base.

Constraint: Goodwill was a meaningful share of the price, and the buyer had few property assets of their own to offer as security.

Structure: Loan from a lender that isn't a bank, repaid over 7 years and secured by business assets, plus a 15% vendor loan (the seller lets the buyer pay part of the price later) that ranks behind the main loan.

Loan $980k · LVR n/a · Lender tier Non-bank · Days to settle 46

Example structure — illustrative

Franchise acquisition, Rockhampton QLD

Situation: An existing franchisee buying a second territory from a retiring operator within the same network.

Constraint: Two territories together increased the lender's exposure to a single brand.

Structure: Loan from a mid-tier bank, secured by residential property, with an earn-out (part of the price paid later, depending on future profits) covering 10% of the price over two years.

Loan $720k · LVR 65% · Lender tier Mid-tier bank · Days to settle 39

Commercial refinance

Example structure — illustrative

Warehouse refinance, Welshpool WA

Situation: A logistics operator facing a rate change on renewal from its current lender, one of the four major banks, six weeks before its facility expired.

Constraint: A short timeframe meant the property valuation and loan approval had to move quickly to avoid rolling over onto the higher rate.

Structure: Refinance from one bank to another, 60% LVR, 15-year term, no break costs (fees for ending a fixed-rate loan early) on the outgoing variable-rate loan.

Loan $2.6m · LVR 60% · Lender tier Major bank · Days to settle 27

Example structure — illustrative

Retail strata refinance, Ipswich QLD

Situation: A retail business that owns and uses its premises, coming off a fixed rate with a lender that isn't a bank and had since tightened its lending rules for retail property.

Constraint: A break cost applied to the outgoing fixed-rate loan, and the property needed a new lender-ordered valuation.

Structure: Refinance with a mid-tier bank, 65% LVR, break cost included in the funds paid at settlement.

Loan $1.05m · LVR 65% · Lender tier Mid-tier bank · Days to settle 34

Structure: Lender that isn't a bank, 70% LVR.

Childcare centre purchase, Maitland

Situation: An investor buying a leased childcare centre operating under a 12-year lease.

Constraint: A specialised type of property that most lenders price and size differently from standard commercial property.

Structure: Mid-tier bank loan at 60% LVR against the specialised property.

Constraint: Existing ATO arrangement narrowed which lenders would consider the deal.

Structure: Specialist lender, 55% LVR.
