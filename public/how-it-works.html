The full process, step by step

Step 1

Enquiry

You complete a short form covering the deal, who is borrowing and your timeframe. No login and no document upload needed at this stage.

Step 2

Written assessment within two business days

We review what you've told us and send a written assessment of how the deal is likely to be viewed by lenders, including the fee tier it's likely to fall under.

Step 3

Lender shortlist and estimated terms

We shortlist lenders whose lending rules fit the deal and set out an estimated interest rate, LVR (loan-to-value ratio — the loan as a % of the property's value) and loan term for each. The lender sets the terms, and they are estimates until credit approval (the lender's formal yes).

Step 4

Application pack

Once you choose a lender to proceed with, we put together the application pack. What we typically need from you:

  • Identification for each person, company or trust involved
  • Documents showing how each borrower is set up — trust deed, company extract or partnership agreement
  • Last two years' financial statements and tax returns
  • Recent bank statements for the borrower
  • Contract of sale, business sale agreement or existing loan letter, as relevant
  • What each guarantor (the person who backs the loan) owns and owes

Step 5

Credit approval

The lender's credit team assesses the application against its own lending rules. We manage any follow-up questions so you're not fielding them directly.

Step 6

Valuation

The lender orders a property valuation on the asset offered as security (the asset the lender can claim if the loan isn't repaid). Final LVR and loan amount are set once the valuation comes back.

Step 7

Loan documents

The lender issues the loan contract and security documents (the paperwork giving it a claim over the asset). We recommend you get your own legal advice before you sign, particularly on your obligations as a guarantor and on security.

Step 8

Settlement

The loan funds are paid out and the deal completes. Our fee, where one applies, is invoiced at this point — never before.

Typical timelines by deal type

Estimates — every deal moves at the pace of how the borrower is structured, its lender and how quickly documents come back.

Streamline

2–4 weeks

Standard purchase or refinance, one borrowing entity, mainstream bank lender.

Structured

4–8 weeks

Several borrowing entities or trust structures, self-managed super fund (SMSF) purchases, non-bank lenders (lenders that aren't banks).

Specialist

6–12 weeks

Past credit problems, development and construction, loans from private lenders.

What can slow a deal down

  • Financial statements or tax returns that are more than 12 months old and need updating
  • Trust, company or partnership structures with documents that need amending before a lender will accept them
  • A valuation that comes in below purchase price or existing loan balance
  • A lease or contract with a related party (such as your own company) that needs to be put on normal commercial terms
  • Guarantors who haven't yet obtained their own legal advice
  • A loan expiry date close enough that there's little room to negotiate terms
