Example structure — illustrative

Industrial purchase, Beresfield NSW

Situation: A trades business buying the 900 m² unit it already operates from, held in a trust with a corporate trustee.

Constraint: Only 18 months of trading history under the current structure — not enough for a major bank.

Structure: Non-bank lender, 65% LVR, 25-year term repaying principal and interest.

Medical suite refinance, Parramatta NSW

Situation: A medical practice whose loan was due to expire in six weeks, after a big-four bank changed its interest rate on renewal.

Constraint: Little time to refinance before the existing loan lapsed.

Structure: Mid-tier bank (smaller than the big four), 60% LVR.

Loan $2.1m · LVR 60% · Lender tier Mid-tier bank · Days to settle 24

Frequently asked

What LVR can I get on commercial property?

It depends on the property type and the lender's rules on the day — typically 55–70% of value. See the borrowing check above for estimated bands.

Do you charge upfront?

No. Streamline deals are $0 to you and payable only at settlement. Structured and Specialist deals have a published fee, also payable at settlement only.

Can my SMSF buy the premises?

Yes, via a limited recourse borrowing arrangement (LRBA) — the special loan type super funds must use. Typical LVR is around 60% and lead times run longer than a standard purchase, so start early.

How fast can you settle?

Timeframes vary with how the borrower is structured (company, trust, several entities) and the lender. Straightforward bank deals move faster; deals with several entities or a non-bank lender can take longer. Ask for a timeframe specific to your deal.

Which locations do you cover?

Australia-wide. Deals are handled remotely end to end; site visits are arranged where a lender requires one.
