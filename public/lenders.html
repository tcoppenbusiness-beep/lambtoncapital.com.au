Mid-tier banks and the big four are planned to follow as our record of completed deals grows — most banks require a track record before granting accreditation. The panel is being finalised, so we don't publish a lender count on this page; see facts & licensing for the current status.

What "lender tier" means in our deal notes

Our deal notes label the lender used in each example as a tier — Major bank, Mid-tier bank, Non-bank or Specialist lender (one focused on harder-to-place deals). This tells you the type of lender the deal was placed with, not a specific institution, since deal notes are anonymised. Major and mid-tier banks generally offer lower interest rates for straightforward deals. Non-bank and specialist lenders generally take deals that fall outside mainstream bank lending rules — shorter trading history, an unusual asset offered as security (the asset the lender can claim if the loan isn't repaid) or a tighter timeframe — at a higher interest rate.
