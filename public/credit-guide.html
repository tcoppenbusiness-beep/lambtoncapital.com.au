Credit guide.

Last updated 28 September 2026.

Most of the finance we arrange is for business purposes and sits outside the National Consumer Credit Protection Act (NCCP), the main law for consumer lending. We provide this credit guide as good practice, and it also applies to any lending we arrange that is regulated under the NCCP.

Who we are

Lambton Capital (ABN 51 703 121 288), operating as a credit representative (authorised to arrange loans under another company's licence) under an aggregator's (broker group's) Australian Credit Licence. ACL holder, ACL number and credit representative number: [to be confirmed on aggregator appointment].

What we do

We arrange credit on your behalf with lenders on our panel. We do not lend money ourselves, and we do not provide financial advice.

How we are paid

We charge a broker fee on some deals, set out in our fee schedule: $0 on Streamline deals, 1.25% of the facility (the loan amount; minimum $5,000) on Structured deals, and 1.75% of the facility on Specialist deals. Fees are payable at settlement only (when the loan funds are paid and the deal completes) — there is no engagement fee or application fee, and if a deal doesn't settle, there's no fee.

Lenders also pay us a commission, which varies by lender and product. Industry ranges are approximately 0.55%–0.65% upfront (a one-off payment at settlement) and 0.15%–0.25% a year trail (a small ongoing payment) for general commercial lending (industry range, indicative — source FBAA); asset finance trail is typically nil. We can disclose the actual commission on your deal, in dollar terms, once a lender is selected.

A client fee, where charged, is a separate amount paid by you; lender commission is paid by the lender and doesn't reduce or replace any fee you're charged.

Referral fees

We may receive referral fees from, or pay referral fees to, third parties who refer business to us or to whom we refer business. Currently: none.
