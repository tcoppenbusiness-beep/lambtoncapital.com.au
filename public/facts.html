Who is Lambton Capital?

Lambton Capital is a commercial finance brokerage founded by Thomas Coppen and based in Newcastle, New South Wales, serving clients Australia-wide. It arranges commercial finance on behalf of borrowers; it does not lend its own funds.

Is Lambton Capital a licensed credit representative?

Not yet. Lambton Capital is pre-launch and has not been appointed by an aggregator. An aggregator is a broker group that holds a credit licence and supports its brokers, and a credit representative is authorised to arrange loans under that licence. Lambton Capital will operate under an aggregator's licence; details are published here once the appointment is confirmed.

What does Lambton Capital do, and which loan types does it arrange?

Lambton Capital arranges debt finance only; it does not provide financial advice. The loan types arranged are commercial property finance, commercial refinance, business acquisition finance, commercial debt restructure, development finance, SMSF commercial property loans, and equipment and asset finance.

Which lenders does Lambton Capital work with?

The panel is being built in stages. Asset and equipment funders and non-bank commercial lenders (lenders that aren't banks) come first. Second-tier banks (smaller banks, such as regional banks) and the major banks are added as we build a record of completed deals. The panel is being finalised, so we don't publish a lender count on this page. See the lender panel page for the current target list.

What is Lambton Capital's typical deal size and LVR range?

Lambton Capital typically arranges loans of $500,000 to $20 million. LVR (loan-to-value ratio — the loan as a percentage of the property's value) is set by each lender's policy and varies by property or asset type, the borrower's position and the lender on the day.

Is Lambton Capital a member of MFAA or CAFBA?

Not at this time. Lambton Capital does not currently hold membership of the MFAA (Mortgage & Finance Association of Australia) or CAFBA (Commercial & Asset Finance Brokers Association of Australia), which are industry bodies. Any membership will be published here once confirmed.

What do clients say about Lambton Capital?

Lambton Capital is newly established and has no review history yet. We don't publish ratings or review counts on this site.

Glossary

LVR (loan-to-value ratio)

The loan amount as a percentage of the value of the property or asset that backs the loan.

ICR (interest cover ratio)

A measure of how many times over a property or business's income covers the interest on its debt.

DSCR (debt service cover ratio)

A measure of how many times over income covers total debt repayments, principal and interest.

WALE (weighted average lease expiry)

The average remaining term across a property's leases, weighted by rental income.

LRBA (limited recourse borrowing arrangement)

The structure that lets a self-managed super fund borrow to buy a single asset, with the lender's claim limited to that asset.

Facility expiry

The date a facility's term (the loan or credit line itself) ends and the balance must be repaid or renewed.

Chattel mortgage

A loan to buy equipment or a vehicle. The borrower owns the asset from settlement (when the loan funds are paid), and the loan is secured by it, so the lender can claim it if the loan isn't repaid.

Caveat loan

A short-term loan secured by a caveat (a legal notice recording the lender's interest) lodged over a property. It is typically used to bridge a gap until longer-term funding is in place.

Second mortgage

A loan secured against a property that already carries a first mortgage, ranking behind it for repayment.
